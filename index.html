📱

Dynamic QR Code Generator

Lecturers generate time-bound, cryptographically signed lecture session QR codes displayed in full-screen projector mode with live countdown timers.

📷

Authenticated Camera Scanner

Students scan the lecture QR code using mobile device cameras with instant verification, accompanied by a 6-character manual code fallback.

⏳

Automated Latecomer Detection

The backend compares check-in timestamps against lecturer-configured late thresholds, automatically categorizing students as Present or Late.

🛡️

Atomic Duplicate Prevention

Enforces atomic transactional guarantees with compound keys (session_id + student_id) ensuring no student can register attendance twice.

🎯 Core Project Objectives (GET 411)

  • Digitize the process of recording lecture attendance across university lecture halls.
  • Allow lecturers to generate unique, secure QR codes for individual lecture sessions.
  • Allow authenticated students to scan QR codes using their mobile or computer cameras.
  • Automatically record trusted server date and time stamps upon verification.
  • Accurately identify students who arrive after the permitted attendance time threshold.
  • Prevent duplicate attendance submissions through atomic database constraints.
  • Provide real-time attendance dashboards and downloadable CSV analytical reports.
